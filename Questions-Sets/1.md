## Quality & Scope

This is a JPSC-oriented static question bank, not a random Jharkhand-GK dump. It concentrates on historical developments, tribal and wider society, movements and personalities, festivals and traditions, languages and literature, folk arts, heritage sites, important places, and stable cultural/historical facts relevant to the official Jharkhand-Specific Awareness syllabus.

The bank was consolidated from the project's researched Jharkhand history, tribal-society, and art/culture/heritage research sets, then deduplicated within each source, standardized into bilingual MCQs, checked for four-option consistency, and balanced for answer positions and difficulty. Tribal-society questions use same-domain distractors where possible and broader tribal-society distractors only when necessary.

The factual base prioritizes Jharkhand Government, Jharkhand Tourism and Census of India material used in the underlying research.

## Coverage

- **History:** prehistoric/archaeological background, Nagvanshi and regional history, colonial period, tribal uprisings, Birsa movement, Tana Bhagat movement, freedom struggle, Jharkhand movement and state formation.
- **Society:** tribes, PVTGs, social organization, religion/belief systems, demography, livelihoods, tribal rights and social institutions.
- **Culture:** festivals, folk traditions, dances, music, musical instruments, languages, scripts, literature and traditional arts.
- **Heritage:** forts, temples, archaeological sites, historic capitals, rock art, cultural landmarks and important tourist/heritage places.
