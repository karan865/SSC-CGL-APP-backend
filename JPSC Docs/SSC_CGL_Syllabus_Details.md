## Stage: Tier 2 (Mains Examination)

### Paper 1 (Compulsory for all posts)
*Type: Objective Multiple Choice | Duration: 2 hours 15 minutes*

#### Section I: Module I - Mathematical Abilities
- Marks: 90
- Questions: 30

**Topics:**
- Number Systems (3 Qs • 9 Marks)
- Fundamental arithmetical operations (5 Qs • 15 Marks)
- Algebra (5 Qs • 15 Marks)
- Geometry (5 Qs • 15 Marks)
- Mensuration (5 Qs • 15 Marks)
- Trigonometry (4 Qs • 12 Marks)
- Statistics and probability (3 Qs • 9 Marks)

#### Section I: Module II - Reasoning and General Intelligence
- Marks: 90
- Questions: 30

**Topics:**
- Semantic Analogy, Symbolic operations, Symbolic/Number Analogy, Trends, Figural Analogy (7 Qs • 21 Marks)
- Space Orientation, Semantic Classification, Venn Diagrams, Symbolic/Number Classification, Drawing inferences, Figural Classification (8 Qs • 24 Marks)
- Punched hole/pattern-folding & unfolding, Semantic Series, Figural Pattern-folding and completion, Number Series, Embedded figures, Figural Series (8 Qs • 24 Marks)
- Critical Thinking, Problem Solving, Emotional Intelligence, Word Building, Social Intelligence, Coding and de-coding (7 Qs • 21 Marks)

#### Section II: Module I - English Language and Comprehension
- Marks: 135
- Questions: 45

**Topics:**
- Vocabulary, grammar, sentence structure, synonyms, antonyms and their correct usage (8 Qs • 24 Marks)
- Spot the Error, Fill in the Blanks, Synonyms/Homonyms, Antonyms, Spellings/Detecting mis-spelt words (8 Qs • 24 Marks)
- Idioms & Phrases, One word substitution, Improvement of Sentences (7 Qs • 21 Marks)
- Active/Passive Voice of Verbs, Conversion into Direct/Indirect narration (7 Qs • 21 Marks)
- Shuffling of Sentence parts, Shuffling of Sentences in a passage (5 Qs • 15 Marks)
- Cloze Passage, Comprehension Passage (10 Qs • 30 Marks)

#### Section II: Module II - General Awareness
- Marks: 75
- Questions: 25

**Topics:**
- Questions designed to test the candidate’s general awareness of the environment around them and its application to society (8 Qs • 24 Marks)
- Knowledge of current events and of such matters of everyday observation and experience (8 Qs • 24 Marks)
- India and its neighboring countries especially pertaining to History, Culture, Geography, Economic Scene, General policy and scientific research (9 Qs • 27 Marks)

#### Section III: Module I - Computer Knowledge Test
- Marks: 60
- Questions: 20

**Topics:**
- Computer Basics: Organization of a computer, Central Processing Unit (CPU), input/output devices, computer memory, memory organization, back-up devices, PORTs, Windows Explorer, Keyboard shortcuts (5 Qs • 15 Marks)
- Software: Windows Operating system including basics of Microsoft Office like MS word, MS Excel and Power Point etc (5 Qs • 15 Marks)
- Working with Internet and e-mails: Web Browsing & Searching, Downloading & Uploading, Managing an E-mail Account, e-Banking (5 Qs • 15 Marks)
- Basics of networking and cyber security: Networking devices and protocols, Network and information security threats (like hacking, virus, worms, Trojan etc.) and preventive measures (5 Qs • 15 Marks)

#### Section III: Module II - Data Entry Speed Test (DEST)
*Qualifying Nature*
- Tasks: 2000 Key Depressions in 15 minutes

### Paper 3: General Studies (Finance and Economics) (Only for Assistant Audit Officer/Assistant Accounts Officer)
*Type: Objective Multiple Choice | Max Marks: 200 | Questions: 100*

#### Part A: Finance and Accounts
- Marks: 80
- Questions: 40

**Topics:**
- Fundamental principles and basic concept of Accounting: Financial Accounting, Nature and scope, Limitations of Financial Accounting, Basic concepts and Conventions, Generally Accepted Accounting Principles (13 Qs • 26 Marks)
- Basic concepts of accounting: Single and double entry, Books of original Entry, Bank Reconciliation, Journal, ledgers, Trial Balance, Rectification of Errors, Manufacturing, Trading, Profit & loss Appropriation Accounts, Balance Sheet (14 Qs • 28 Marks)
- Distinction between Capital and Revenue Expenditure, Depreciation Accounting, Valuation of Inventories, Non-profit organisations Accounts, Receipts and Payments and Income & Expenditure Accounts, Bills of Exchange, Self Balancing Ledgers (13 Qs • 26 Marks)

#### Part B: Economics and Governance
- Marks: 120
- Questions: 60

**Topics:**
- Comptroller & Auditor General of India- Constitutional provisions, Role and responsibility (4 Qs • 8 Marks)
- Finance Commission-Role and functions (4 Qs • 8 Marks)
- Basic Concept of Economics and introduction to Micro Economics (6 Qs • 12 Marks)
- Theory of Demand and Supply (6 Qs • 12 Marks)
- Theory of Production and cost (6 Qs • 12 Marks)
- Forms of Market and price determination in different markets (6 Qs • 12 Marks)
- Indian Economy: Nature, Population, Poverty, Unemployment, Infrastructure (8 Qs • 16 Marks)
- Economic Reforms in India: Economic reforms since 1991; Liberalisation, Privatisation, Globalisation and Disinvestment (6 Qs • 12 Marks)
- Money and Banking: Monetary/Fiscal policy, RBI, Commercial Banks, SEBI, Functions of Money, Inflation (9 Qs • 18 Marks)
- Role of Information Technology in Governance (5 Qs • 10 Marks)
